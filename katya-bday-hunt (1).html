The hunt

Photo prompts

10 – 13

photos across the whole weekend. One per prompt, plus candids, blurry ones, weird ones — all of it counts. 13 is the number of the day, so shoot for it.

  • 01

    Joint in a hot tub

    Katya in the hot tub, joint in hand, steam rising off the water. That's it. That's the prompt.

    Capture the unbothered energy. She's earned it.

    Friday night
  • 02

    Any meal

    Get a shot of the table before anyone digs in — the whole spread, the faces, the anticipation. Or just the most beautiful thing on your plate.

    Any meal, any day. Thai night is a strong contender.

    Any meal
  • 03

    The candy salad

    Document the chaotic glory of everyone's gummies combined into one bowl. Get in close. Let it be a little unhinged.

    The full bowl, or a single perfect gummy held up to the light.

    Friday dessert
  • 04

    Take a scenic drive

    Shoot through the window — blurry trees, open road, the horizon doing its thing. Movement is the point.

    Don't wait for a perfect view. The imperfect one is better.

    Saturday drive
  • 05

    A beach find — sea glass, shell, or pottery

    Lay your find on the sand or hold it open in your palm, up close.

    Pottery shard = jackpot. A plain rock you love = equally valid.

    Beach walk
  • 06

    A fun drink

    Coffee, wine, cocktail — photograph it like it's the star of its own little story. Condensation, steam, a good garnish.

    Any time, any day. Don't overthink it.

    Any time
  • 07

    A thrift or trinket that speaks to you

    Find something that fits in your palm anywhere in Portland — a shop, a market, a stoop sale — and photograph it like it's precious. Because it is now.

    Hold it up, lay it in light, or tuck it into your journal later.

    Anywhere in Portland
  • 08

    An ice cream cone

    The cone, the scoop, the sky behind it. Or someone mid-lick if they'll let you.

    Maine in May means you've earned it.

    Saturday treat
  • 09

    A flower

    Find a flower — growing wild, fallen on pavement, tucked into a window box or a fence — and photograph it like it deserves to be remembered.

    In May in Maine, you won't have to look far.

    Anywhere
  • 10

    The junk journal spread

    Photograph your ephemera laid out flat before it gets glued down — your little archive. Then shoot your finished page.

    Two moments of the same prompt: before and after.

    Saturday night

Bonus challenges

  • The green shot — find something fully, gloriously green and make it your whole frame. Pine bough, painted wall, a sweater. Anything.
  • The unexpected one — take one photo of something nobody planned, that you'll all remember anyway.
  • A group shot — everyone in frame, however you can manage it. Messy, mid-laugh, slightly chaotic. The one you'll actually want to keep.
